# Community conduct

This project welcomes contributors with different languages, backgrounds, and experience levels. Discuss explanations, code, and evidence respectfully.

- Give concrete feedback and help identify missing prerequisites.
- Address the work rather than making personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's confidential information.
- Do not harass, threaten, or exclude people for personal characteristics.
- Keep issue and review discussions relevant to the project.

The maintainer may ask for changes, moderate content, or limit participation when these expectations are repeatedly violated. Concerns can be raised with the maintainer through the GitHub profile linked in README.md; do not post private details in public issues.

This policy is original project documentation under MIT, not a copy of a third-party code-of-conduct template.
